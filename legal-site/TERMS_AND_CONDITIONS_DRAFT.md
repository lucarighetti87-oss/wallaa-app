## Service purpose
Wallaa is a personal-safety communication tool that lets a user send alerts and location information to selected trusted contacts. Wallaa is not a public emergency service and does not replace 112.

## No guaranteed response
Delivery depends on the user's device, Bluetooth accessory, network connectivity, iOS permissions, APNs/email providers and guardian availability. Wallaa does not guarantee delivery, monitoring or intervention.

## Wallaa Operating Center
For Wallaa Pro accounts, a real SOS may be copied to the Wallaa Operating Center safety inbox. Wallaa Basic does not include this forwarding feature. Unless separately contracted and explicitly stated, this does not create a guaranteed monitoring, dispatch or emergency-response service.

## User responsibilities
Users must provide accurate account information, keep credentials secure, configure guardians responsibly, obtain any necessary permission before entering another person's contact details, and test the system.

## Hardware
Bluetooth range, battery state, radio conditions and iOS background behavior can affect accessory availability. Users should maintain and periodically test the Wallaa Button.

## Wallaa Basic and Wallaa Pro
Wallaa Basic supports up to two personal SOS contacts and sends the initial SOS location when location sharing is enabled. Wallaa Pro is designed to add expanded safety features, including up to five personal SOS contacts, Wallaa Operating Center forwarding and movement live tracking. Any paid subscription must be offered through the applicable App Store purchase mechanism before it is enabled for public purchase.

## Safety word
The safety word is a communication aid for trusted contacts. It does not guarantee identification of coercion or emergency response.

## Account deletion
Users may initiate deletion in the app. Data not legally required to be retained is deleted according to the Privacy Policy.
