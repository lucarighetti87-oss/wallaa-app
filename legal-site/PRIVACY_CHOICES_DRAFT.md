# WALLAA — Privacy Choices

Users can manage Wallaa privacy through the app:

- Settings → Privacy Center: review data use and legal documents.
- Safety Network / Contacts: control SOS Alerts, Live Location and Disconnect Alerts per guardian.
- Settings → Essential Wallaa: enable or disable SOS location sharing.
- iOS Settings: control Location, Bluetooth, Camera and Notifications permissions.
- Settings → Delete Account: initiate permanent account deletion.
- Requests: info@wallaa.it.

Wallaa does not use personal-safety data for advertising tracking.
